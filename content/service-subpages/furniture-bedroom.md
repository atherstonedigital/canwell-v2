intro_h2: "The bedroom range"
intro_body: |
  **Beds.** Frames in oak, walnut, and upholstered styles. From singles for guest rooms through superkings for primary bedrooms. Headboards in fabric, leather, or wood.

  **Wardrobes.** Freestanding wardrobes in solid hardwood, with hanging space and internal shelving. From single doors to triples. We don't do fitted wardrobes, but we know a good local fitter if that's what you need.

  **Chests of drawers and tallboys.** Solid wood construction. From two-drawer bedsides to seven-drawer tallboys.

  **Bedside tables.** Sized to take a lamp, a book, a glass of water, and not much else. Available with drawers, with shelves, or as a plain side table.

  **Dressing tables and stools.** A small but rotating range, often in painted finishes.

  A note on older houses: cottage bedrooms reward furniture one size down from what the catalogue suggests. A low-ceilinged room takes a lower bed frame and a pair of slim bedsides far better than a tall sleigh bed, and the stairs matter as much as the room — we've carried enough wardrobes up cottage staircases to know which pieces make the turn and which don't. Measure the staircase before you fall for anything tall.
